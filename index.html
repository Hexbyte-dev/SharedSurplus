Our mission

Shared Surplus rescues new and like-new goods that would otherwise go to waste and gives them a second life. We sell them live, on camera, so you can see exactly what you're getting.

Every purchase sends a portion of the money back into our community. We use it to buy new essentials for local groups serving families, seniors, and neighbors in need across Lancaster, Harrisburg, and York.
